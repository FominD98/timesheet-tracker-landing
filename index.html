FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Timesheet Tracker an employee monitoring tool?

No. It is built for the person who logs the time. There are no screenshots, no keystroke logging, no manager dashboards and no team reports. Your activity stays on your PC, and only the worklogs you confirm are sent to Jira.

What data leaves my computer?

Nothing, until you act. When you click Sort my day, masked window titles, app names, domains and durations of the unassigned blocks are sent to the AI. Full URLs, page content, screenshots, keystrokes and your Jira token are never sent. When you confirm the day, the worklogs you approved go to your Jira. Details are in the privacy policy.

Can I use it without AI?

Yes. In No-AI mode nothing is sent to an AI provider. Jira keys in window titles, URLs and git branches are still matched locally, and you assign the remaining blocks yourself in the review screen.

Which Jira versions does it support?

Jira Cloud and Jira Server / Data Center. Worklogs are written with your own account, and re-sending a day does not create duplicates. New to worklogs? See how to log time in Jira.

How does it know which Jira task I worked on?

Most of the day is matched without AI: Jira keys such as ABC-123 appear in window titles, browser tabs, pull request names and git branch names like feature/ABC-123-login. AI only handles the leftovers, and you confirm every assignment before anything is logged.

Does it work with Tempo or Excel?

Today it writes native Jira worklogs and exports CSV. Direct Tempo support and Excel (XLSX) export are planned.

How much does it cost?

Timesheet Tracker is free during early access. If paid plans appear later, they will be announced in advance and early users will hear first.

When can I download it?

The app is almost ready and is coming to the Microsoft Store. Join the waitlist and we will send you one email when it is live.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Timesheet Tracker is a Windows app. Tell us at help@timesheet-tracker.com if you need another platform.
